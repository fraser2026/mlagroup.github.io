Process

From diagnostic
to board report.

Three steps from completion to a defensible, audit-ready governance assessment.

01
Complete the Diagnostic

Answer 35 questions across seven governance domains — inventory, accountability, data quality, oversight, explainability, compliance, and resilience. Takes approximately 20 minutes.

02
Receive Your Risk Score

Your responses are scored against our weighted framework, adjusted for sector exposure. You receive an overall maturity score, section breakdown, and risk band — immediately on completion.

A full 8-page PDF is generated — including named findings with regulatory basis, impact analysis, and prioritised recommendations. Board-ready. Auditor-ready. Yours to keep.
