The system, applied.

Two primitives — the Anchor Line and the Compliance Bar — extend RGA-001 across every product surface. The first is structural. The second is functional. Together they carry the brand from the front door of the website to the seal of every certificate. Nothing else is needed.

00Primitives

Two elements. One accent.

Every surface in the RegAnchor system is built from two primitives and the six neutrals defined in RGA-001. A single accent gradient — used only inside these two primitives — is the only colour that ever appears in the brand.

00.1Anchor Line · 1px ink with 80px gradient segment at 61.8%structural
Geometry
1px ink hairline, full width of parent surface. Replaced by a 2px gradient segment, 80px wide, centred at 61.8% (golden ratio) from the left edge.
Accent gradient
linear-gradient(90deg, #3730A3, #7C3AED 50%, #F97316)
Animation
Once per page on first paint. Segment fades in horizontally over 1200ms with cubic-bezier(.22, 1, .36, 1). No looping.
Function
Structural baseline. Section divider. Header / footer rule. Form underline.
Placement
Beneath header on every page. Between sections of long-form documents. Beneath certificate IDs.
00.2Compliance Bar · 7 levels, current state at gradientfunctional
Standard · 120px
Mini · 60px
Geometry
7 horizontal bars, 4px tall, 3px gap, stacked column-reverse (lowest level at bottom). Standard width 120px, mini width 60px (for table rows, navbar).
Encoding
Bottom rows = ink at 88%, 64%, 44% opacity (achieved levels). Current row = accent gradient, 5px tall, 130px wide (visually punches out). Top rows = ink at 20%, 10%, 5% (pending).
Levels
L1 Initial · L2 Aware · L3 Defined · L4 Structured · L5 Managed · L6 Optimised · L7 Authoritative. Maps to score bands of ~14 points each (0–100 scale).
Function
Single-glance read of governance maturity. Used wherever a system, organisation, or assessment has a level. Never decorative.
Pairing
Always sits adjacent to the numeric score and tier label. Bar carries the visual; text carries the precision.

The gradient appears only inside these two primitives. It does not appear in body copy, headings, buttons, badges, charts, or as a decorative accent anywhere else. This is the discipline that earns it weight.

ASystem rules

Six constraints. Absolute.

Restraint compounds. These rules govern when and where the gradient appears anywhere in the brand and product.

01 · Two surfaces only
The gradient appears inside the Anchor Line and the Compliance Bar. Nowhere else. Not in buttons, badges, charts, illustrations, headings, or icons.
02 · One Anchor Line per page
The gradient segment fades in once on first paint. After that it is static. No looping, no breathing, no hover state.
03 · One Compliance Bar per context
The dashboard shows the organisational bar once. The registry shows one bar per row. The dossier shows one bar at the seal. The diagnostic result shows one bar beside the score.
04 · Always paired with text
The Compliance Bar is never deployed alone. It is always adjacent to the numeric score and the named tier. The bar carries the visual; the text carries the precision.
05 · No flat accent
The accent gradient is never flattened to a single colour for any use. No solid indigo button. No solid coral badge. The three-stop gradient is the system primitive — its constituent colours are not.
06 · Ink-on-paper everywhere else
Every other surface element follows RGA-001 strictly. Six neutrals. No exceptions.
