The governance challenge
01
Boards are being asked to evidence AI decisions they cannot currently account for.
Regulatory supervisory engagement is testing board-level understanding of AI deployment across financial services, healthcare, and professional services. Most organisations lack the structured documentation to respond with confidence.
02
Governance obligations now apply across multiple frameworks simultaneously.
UK GDPR, FCA DP5/22, and EU AI Act obligations overlap and compound. Few organisations have mapped their exposure across all three in a single, coherent documented position.

Operational AI adoption has moved faster than governance frameworks in most regulated organisations. The diagnostic identifies precisely where that gap exists — and quantifies how significant it is at the point of assessment.
