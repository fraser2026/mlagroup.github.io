Strata.

RegAnchor's premium object. Built from the same six tones as the wordmark — nothing added, nothing borrowed from Vanta, Swan, or Stripe except the standard of craft.

01The object

Move your cursor over it. Scroll the page.

A tapering stack of seven cut facets, base to summit. Tilts toward the cursor, drifts slightly as you scroll, settles when you stop. Every tone is lifted directly from the palette in RGA-001 — the object is the palette, just given form.

02Why this form

Seven courses. Not decoration — a count that means something.

Grok's version was a faceted crystal: well-made, but arbitrary — nothing in its shape referred back to RegAnchor specifically. It could sit on any SaaS homepage in the world. Strata is built from decisions that only make sense here.

Seven courses
Matches the Compliance Bar's seven levels exactly. Not a metaphor — the same number, reused on purpose, so the object and the product UI are counting the same thing.
Base → summit
The bottom course is Ink, the top is Paper — the object travels through all six brand tones in order. Bedrock at the base, clarity at the top: the read is "grounded, then resolved," which is the whole pitch of an assurance product.
No accent colour
Zero hex values outside the palette in RGA-001. I didn't extend the Anchor Line gradient onto it — that gradient is scoped to two named primitives in your own rules, and quietly adding a third felt like the kind of drift that erodes a restrained system. If you want Strata to carry the gradient as a hover state, that's worth deciding on deliberately rather than me assuming it.
No mark, still true to rule 4
It's not standing in for a logo and never appears beside the wordmark the way an icon would — it's closer to a material finish than a symbol, the way Vanta's rock is texture, not their logo. Worth codifying in RGA-001 once you're happy with it, so it's an intentional exception rather than an unofficial one.
03Scattered

One object, cropped and scaled — not four different graphics.

Everything below reuses the exact same SVG. That's the point of building it as one asset: it stays recognisably the same object no matter where it lands, the way Vanta's rock is legible whether it's 40px or 400px.
