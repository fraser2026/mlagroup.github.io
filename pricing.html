Frequently asked.

Can I start free and upgrade later?

Yes. Your governance data, control progress, and assessments are preserved when you upgrade. Move to Essentials or Professional at any time from your portal dashboard.

What qualifies me for a governance certificate?

An active Essentials or Professional subscription, a governance score of 70% or above, at least one registered AI system with a completed assessment, and all published policies acknowledged. Your portal tracks qualification progress in real time.

What happens if I cancel?

Your portal access and governance data remain intact. Your certificate will expire, public verification will be deactivated, and PDF reports will be locked. Resubscribe at any time to reinstate.

Is the certificate publicly verifiable?

Yes. Every active certificate has a unique ID and can be verified by anyone on our public verification page. The page confirms the organisation, level, score, and validity period.

Can I switch between monthly and annual?

Yes. Contact us to switch your billing period. Annual plans save approximately 17% compared to monthly.

Which regulatory frameworks are covered?

The EU AI Act, UK GDPR, FCA DP5/22, ICO AI Guidance, and ISO/IEC 42001. Every finding includes specific article references and regulatory citations.
