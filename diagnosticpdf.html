Section 2 of 7
Governance, Leadership & Accountability
Assessing the maturity of internal ownership and oversight structures.
2.1Are AI systems clearly classified by risk level within your organisation?
2.2Is there a formally appointed individual or function responsible for AI governance?
2.3Does executive leadership receive regular reporting on AI risk and performance?
2.4Are documented policies in place governing the use of AI systems?
2.5Are roles and responsibilities for AI oversight explicitly defined across risk, compliance, and technical functions?
Section 3 of 7
Data Quality, Lineage & Management
Confirming that your AI systems are supported by clear, auditable data practices.
3.1Is the origin and source of data used by AI systems documented and accessible?
i.e. where data comes from, how it is collected and processed
3.2Do you maintain records of data lineage for core AI systems?
Sources, transformations, and version history
3.3Do you perform regular data quality assessments for inputs feeding AI decisions?
3.4Is there a documented process for identifying and resolving data quality issues?
Section 4 of 7
Human Oversight & Decision Controls
Evaluating how human judgement integrates with AI-driven decisions.
4.1Do AI decision processes include human review for high-impact outcomes?
4.2Are override protocols documented for when a human reviewer disagrees with an AI outcome?
4.3Do staff responsible for reviewing AI decisions receive adequate and documented training?
4.4Are escalation pathways defined for decisions that require additional review or challenge?
Section 5 of 7
Explainability & Transparency
Assessing whether AI-assisted decisions are explainable, traceable, and defensible.
5.1Can your organisation provide clear explanations for AI-assisted decisions when required by an individual or regulator?
5.2Do you maintain logs of AI input features and decision outputs?
5.3Are decision logic and model versions recorded and accessible for audit?
5.4Can a specific AI decision be traced back to its source inputs and the model version used?
Section 6 of 7
Compliance & Legal Alignment
Confirming awareness and alignment with current UK and EU legal expectations.
6.1Has your organisation conducted a formal Data Protection Impact Assessment (DPIA) for AI systems that handle personal data?
6.2Are individuals informed when automated or AI-assisted decisions are made that affect them?
6.3Is there a documented process allowing individuals to request a review or challenge an AI-assisted decision?
Section 7 of 7
Operational Controls & Resilience
Assessing the robustness of risk controls and operational resilience around AI systems.
7.1Are AI systems included in your organisation’s formal risk and control self-assessments?
7.2Do third-party AI providers undergo structured risk assessment and contractual controls review?
7.3Are cybersecurity and adversarial vulnerabilities assessed for AI systems?
e.g. prompt injection, model manipulation, data poisoning
7.4Do you monitor AI model performance and detect drift or degradation over time?
