We do not use advertising cookies, tracking pixels, or third-party analytics.

Section 02

What Cookies Are

A cookie is a small text file placed on your device by a website. Related technologies — including local storage and session storage — store data in your browser in a similar way. This policy covers all of them, because the law treats them the same way regardless of the technical mechanism.

Storage is described as strictly necessary when the service you have asked for cannot function without it. Strictly necessary storage does not require your consent. Everything else does.

Section 03

What We Use

All of the storage set by this website falls into the strictly necessary category.

Section 04

What We Do Not Use

We do not operate Google Analytics or any equivalent analytics platform. We do not use advertising or retargeting cookies. We do not use social media tracking pixels. We do not sell or share browsing data with third parties for marketing purposes.

Because we set no non-essential storage, this site does not present a cookie consent banner. If that changes, we will introduce a consent mechanism before setting any such cookie, and we will update this policy first.

Section 05

Third Parties

Stripe processes payments on our behalf and sets its own cookies for fraud prevention and to complete a checkout. These are governed by Stripe's own privacy and cookie policies.

Supabase provides our database and authentication. Session data is held in your browser's local storage rather than in cookies.

Google Fonts serves the typefaces used on this site. Requesting a font discloses your IP address to Google but sets no cookie.

Section 06

Managing Storage

You can delete or block cookies and local storage through your browser settings. Every major browser allows this, usually under privacy or site data settings.

Because the storage described above is strictly necessary, blocking it will prevent parts of the service from working. In particular, you will not be able to stay signed in to the portal, your diagnostic answers will be lost between pages, and payments may not complete.
