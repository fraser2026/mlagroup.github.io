Changelog.

New capabilities, improvements and fixes to RegAnchor, published as they ship.

Get updates by email Read the docs

18 updates

17 October 2026
NewImproved

Asset usage history and evaluation runs

Each AI asset's Connection tab now brings provider usage and gateway activity together, backed by daily usage history. Evaluation runs appear as a column in the registry, so testing status sits alongside every asset.

  • Daily usage history for each AI asset
  • Usage and gateway cards on the Connection tab
  • Evals column in the registry
  • Clearer empty states and notices in light and dark mode
12 October 2026
New

Custom framework controls

Build your own controls inside custom frameworks. A full control editor, owner assignments and separation from the standard catalogue let you govern internal policy alongside regulation, in one place.

12 October 2026
ImprovedFixed

Risk diagnostic report

The risk diagnostic PDF has a new design, and the diagnostic itself is more reliable.

  • Redesigned diagnostic PDF report
  • Fixed an issue where the diagnostic could stop responding at step two when a required question was missed
12 October 2026
Improved

Navigation and mobile

RegAnchor is easier to move around, on a desk or on a phone.

  • Off-canvas navigation on phones, with rows that wrap instead of overflowing
  • One-line breadcrumbs and a wrapping registry toolbar on small screens
  • Recently visited pages in the sidebar
  • Sidebar groups open by default
  • UK date formats and sentence case throughout
11 October 2026
New

Help and support in the app

A Help button now sits on every page of RegAnchor. Choose a topic, describe the issue and attach files up to 10 MB. The page you were on is included automatically, and you receive an email acknowledgement. See Get support.

11 October 2026
New

Expert evidence review

Evidence you upload can be reviewed by the RegAnchor team. Each item is marked accepted or changes requested, with a note explaining why, and a governance alert tells you when a review needs your attention.

4 October 2026
NewChanged

My work and control assignments

My work lists the controls assigned to you, and assignees receive an email when a control is assigned. Renewals coming due are surfaced in the controls catalogue.

  • Control assignments now save when you select Save, rather than automatically
  • Viewers can no longer be assigned controls, as they cannot change records
4 October 2026
ImprovedFixed

Invitations and onboarding

Inviting your team is faster and more dependable.

  • Invitation emails are delivered through our transactional email service
  • Invitees without a password are guided through account setup, with password confirmation
  • Members are shown by first and last name throughout
  • Fixed an issue where resending a revoked invitation did not always send the email
3 October 2026
New

AI policy drafting, in beta

Draft AI policies with MLA, RegAnchor's drafting assistant. The conversation sits on one side and an editable document on the other. Drafts stream in with tables, support undo and redo, and publish with title and version checks so policy lineage stays intact.

  • Drafting uses AI credits, included with paid plans
  • Each conversation is scoped to the policy you're drafting
24 September 2026
New

Dossier review and e-signature

Governance dossiers can be reviewed and signed inside RegAnchor. Signatures are embedded in the exported PDF, every review and signing step is written to an audit trail, and signed dossiers are listed in Reports.

23 September 2026
New

Auditor access

Give external auditors a time-limited engagement with a scope you control, without adding them to your workspace. Access can be revoked at any time. Available on Professional and above.

18 September 2026
New

Governance owners

Every AI asset now has three named owners: business, compliance and technical. Accountability is clear for each system, and contact details stay in the app rather than in dossier exports.

16 September 2026
New

AI governance dossier and methodology versions

Export an AI governance dossier as a PDF, covering your AI assets, risk and controls, with every high-risk asset listed in the observations. Our methodology is now published as versioned snapshots, so a dossier can cite the exact version it was assessed against.

12 September 2026
NewSecurity

Connect, API keys and two-factor authentication

Connect brings provider connections together at organisation level, alongside API keys and the foundation for data backends.

  • Two-factor authentication with an authenticator app, with recovery codes
  • Keys and environment variables stored in an encrypted vault, with no direct access from the browser
7 September 2026
New

MCP server

Connect MCP-compatible AI assistants to RegAnchor. Sign-in uses OAuth with a consent screen, so assistants act with your permissions and you stay in control of what they can reach.

4 September 2026
New

Governed Anthropic gateway

Route Anthropic requests through the RegAnchor gateway with a key for each AI asset, so usage and cost are attributed to the right system automatically.

3 September 2026
New

AI asset registry

Register every AI system, agent and model, with model, platform and lifecycle details. Connect Anthropic to bring usage insights into each asset.

31 August 2026
Security

Email and account protections

Strengthened protections against sender spoofing in our email service, and tightened the checks used when linking accounts.
