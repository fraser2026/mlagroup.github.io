Obligations are
current.

The EU AI Act’s transparency obligations are now in force, with high-risk requirements under Annex III phased in from December 2027. Alongside that, UK and EU regulators have spent several years building an enforcement record under GDPR, FCA, and ICO frameworks. The kind of record a board is expected to know about.

Built to withstand
scrutiny.

Every element of the platform is designed for organisations operating under active UK and EU regulatory oversight, from assessment logic through to reporting.

Precise Regulatory Mapping

Every finding references the specific article, ICO guidance paragraph, or FCA expectation it addresses, every time — for example, EU AI Act Article 14 on human oversight.

Sector Exposure Adjustment

Your maturity score is adjusted by a sector-specific multiplier, so a financial services deployment and a public sector one are held to different baselines.

EU AI Act Annex III Detection

Automatic identification of high-risk AI categories under Annex III, with conformity assessment flags and Article 14 human oversight obligations — mapped directly from your responses.

Board and Auditor-Ready Output

The generated report is structured for professional audiences — executive summary, risk band narrative, domain-by-domain findings, prioritised recommendations, and a regulatory framework appendix.

Secure Client Portal

Every AI system, control, and certificate lives in your organisation’s private account, not ours to share. Come back any time to check status, pull a report, or see how your posture has moved since the last review.
