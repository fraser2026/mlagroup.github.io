Governance should be operational, not theoretical.

Knowing what exists is not governance. Governance is what happens next. Policies and regulatory requirements only create value when they translate into actions that someone can own, complete and evidence.

RegAnchor connects requirements to the systems, people and controls responsible for meeting them.
