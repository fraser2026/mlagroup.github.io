Security overview

Architecture, hosting boundaries and access controls for customer data.

Architecture

RegAnchor is a modern web application served globally through Cloudflare's edge network and backed by a managed Postgres database on Supabase in the EU. Privileged database operations run only in server-side functions; browsers hold a publishable key, never a privileged service credential.

Hosting and data residency

All customer data and governance records are stored in the European Union (Ireland). Cloudflare provides global application delivery and DDoS mitigation, and PDF documents are generated on Render in the EU (Frankfurt). Our marketing website is hosted separately and holds no customer data.

Encryption

In transit. All traffic is forced over HTTPS and encrypted with TLS 1.2 or higher.

At rest. Databases, file storage and backups are encrypted with AES-256.

Secrets. AI provider keys you connect are held in an isolated, encrypted secrets vault. They are decrypted only inside short-lived server-side functions and never reach the browser.

Authentication and access

Users sign in with a work email and password, or with Google Workspace single sign-on. Two-factor authentication with an authenticator app is available, with recovery codes. Sessions use short-lived, cryptographically signed tokens.

Workspace invitations are single-use links, stored hashed, that expire after 14 days and only work for the invited email address. SAML single sign-on is available on Enterprise.

Tenant isolation

Every table in the database enforces Postgres row-level security. Queries for AI assets, assessments, members and audit records are scoped to workspace membership, so one customer cannot read another's data.

Role-based access control

Workspace roles (Workspace admin, Admin, Editor and Viewer) are enforced in the database rather than the browser, so a user cannot raise their own access. Critical governance actions are written to an append-only audit log.

Data processing

Data flows, subprocessors, retention and deletion.

Data scope

We process essential account details (names and work email addresses) and the governance records created in RegAnchor: AI assets, assessments, controls, policies, evidence and dossiers.

AI and gateway data flows

When you connect an AI provider, RegAnchor reads usage and billing information with your organisation's own API key. Requests routed through the RegAnchor gateway are forwarded to the provider you choose, under your own agreement with that provider.

Retention and deletion

Account data. Kept for the life of the account plus two years.

Governance records. Assessment responses, policy snapshots and dossiers are kept for the life of the account plus three years, for regulatory continuity.

Deletion. At the end of each retention period, or on a verified request to info@reganchor.com, customer data is permanently deleted or anonymised. Full details are in our privacy notice.

Backups

The production database is backed up automatically. Backups are encrypted at rest and held on redundant storage in the same EU region as the live data, protecting against data loss.

Enterprise contracts can add higher-frequency backups, point-in-time recovery, extended retention for audit and dedicated storage on AWS.

Operational resilience

System availability, disaster recovery and incident response.

Incident response

We maintain an incident response process covering detection, containment and remediation. If a security event affects your organisation's personal data, we notify your workspace admins without undue delay, and report to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) where the law requires.

Availability commitment

RegAnchor targets 99.9% monthly uptime. The application runs on Cloudflare's edge network and the database on managed infrastructure, with no single self-operated server to fail.

Disaster recovery

Encrypted backups allow the database to be restored quickly, and point-in-time recovery is available on enterprise contracts. Application code and database schema are fully version-controlled, so the infrastructure can be redeployed reproducibly, including in another region if required.

Support

Security and technical support is available Monday to Friday during UK business hours at support@reganchor.com. More on Get support.

Product and audit assurance

How findings, compliance scores and governance reports are derived.

Methodology and citations

Assessment logic follows published legislation and recognised risk frameworks. Findings carry explicit citations, such as EU AI Act articles and ISO/IEC 42001 clauses. The full approach is published in our methodology.

Framework mapping

Evidence and controls map across the EU AI Act, ISO/IEC 42001, NIST AI RMF, FCA DP5/22 and ICO guidance, so one piece of evidence supports several obligations without duplicate collection.

Append-only audit logs

Governance lifecycle events, such as creating an asset, scoring risk and signing off an assessment, are written to an append-only log that records the user, the time and the change made. Entries cannot be edited from the application.

Scope and limitations

RegAnchor provides governance software and automated assessment tools. It is not legal advice, and it is not accredited certification or audit. Scores and findings depend on the information your organisation provides. See our service terms.
