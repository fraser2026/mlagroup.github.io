Security

RegAnchor runs on Cloudflare's edge network with a managed Postgres database (Supabase, EU). Privileged operations run only in server-side functions.

Infrastructure

  • Customer data and governance records stored in the EU (Supabase, eu-west-1, Ireland)
  • Global delivery, firewall and DDoS mitigation by Cloudflare
  • PDF reports generated in the EU (Render, Frankfurt)
  • Marketing website hosted separately, with no customer data

Encryption

  • HTTPS enforced; TLS 1.2 or higher in transit
  • AES-256 at rest for databases, file storage and backups
  • Connected AI provider keys held in an encrypted secrets vaultDecrypted only in short-lived server-side functions; never sent to the browser.
  • Browsers hold a publishable key only, never a privileged credential

Access control

Who can sign in, what they can see, and how changes are recorded.

Authentication

  • Work email and password, or Google Workspace SSO
  • Authenticator-app two-factor authentication with recovery codes
  • Short-lived, cryptographically signed session tokens
  • Single-use invitations, stored hashed, valid 14 days for the invited address only
  • SAML SSO available on Enterprise plans

Isolation and roles

  • Postgres row-level security on customer data tables, scoped to workspace membership
  • Roles (Workspace admin, Admin, Editor, Viewer) enforced in the database, not the browser
  • Users cannot raise their own access
  • Critical governance actions written to an append-only audit log

Data processing

What we process, where it goes, and how long we keep it.

Scope and flows

  • Account details: names and work email addresses
  • Governance records: AI assets, assessments, controls, policies, evidence and dossiers
  • Connected AI providers are read with your own API key, under your own agreement
  • Gateway requests are forwarded to the provider you choose

Retention and deletion

  • Account data: life of the account plus two years
  • Governance records: life of the account plus three years, for regulatory continuity
  • Deleted or anonymised at the end of retention, or on verified request
  • Requests to security@reganchor.com; details in our privacy notice

International transfers. Some sub-processors, including our AI model provider, may process limited data outside the UK and EU. These transfers are covered by Standard Contractual Clauses and the UK International Data Transfer Addendum.

Resilience

Availability, backups, incident response and support.

Availability and recovery

  • Historical uptime of 99.9%+
  • Application served from Cloudflare's edge on managed infrastructure
  • Automated, encrypted backups in the same EU region as live data
  • Code and schema version-controlled for reproducible redeployment
  • Enterprise plans: higher-frequency backups, point-in-time recovery, extended retention

Incidents and support

  • Documented process for detection, containment and remediation
  • Workspace admins notified without undue delay if their personal data is affected
  • Reports to the ICO where the law requires
  • Support Monday to Friday, UK business hours, via Get support
  • Report a vulnerability to security@reganchor.com

Product assurance

How findings, scores and governance reports are derived.

Method

  • Assessment logic follows published legislation and recognised frameworks
  • Findings cite sources, such as EU AI Act articles and ISO/IEC 42001 clauses
  • Approach published in our methodology

Mapping and records

  • Controls and evidence mapped to the EU AI Act, ISO/IEC 42001, NIST AI RMF, FCA DP5/22 and ICO guidance
  • Append-only log of lifecycle events: user, time and change
  • Log entries cannot be edited from the application

Scope. RegAnchor is governance software. It is not legal advice, and it is not an accredited certification or audit. Results depend on the information your organisation provides. See our service terms.
